How do I reset my password?
To reset your password, go to Settings > Account > Reset Password. You will receive an email with a reset link valid for 24 hours.

How do I export my data?
You can export your data by going to Settings > Data > Export. Choose CSV or JSON format. The export will be emailed to you within 1 hour.

App keeps crashing on startup
Try these steps: 1) Clear the app cache in Settings > Storage. 2) Update to the latest version. 3) Reinstall the app. If the issue persists, contact support.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. Your access continues until the end of the billing period. No refunds for partial months.

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and bank transfers for annual plans.

How long do refunds take?
Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days. The time depends on your payment method and bank.

What are your business hours?
Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM EST. Premium customers have 24/7 access.

How do I upgrade my plan?
Go to Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Select your new plan. The price difference is charged immediately. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

Account locked after too many attempts
Your account is temporarily locked for 30 minutes after 5 failed login attempts. You can use the "Forgot Password" option to reset immediately.

How do I contact support?
You can reach us via email at support@company.com, live chat on our website, or call 1-800-SUPPORT during business hours.
